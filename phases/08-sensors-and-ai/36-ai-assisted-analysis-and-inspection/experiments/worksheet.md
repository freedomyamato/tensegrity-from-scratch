# Lesson 36 worksheet

Topic: AI-assisted analysis and inspection

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Give an assistant a non-identifying member table or use the supplied faulty-example scenario.
- [ ] Ask it to explain the load path and list assumptions.
- [ ] Independently verify one unit conversion and one force calculation.
- [ ] Classify each statement as observed, calculated, inferred or unsupported.
- [ ] Revise the report while retaining disagreements and missing evidence.

## After

Required evidence: An AI-output review with independent corrections.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
