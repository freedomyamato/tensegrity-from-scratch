# Lesson 33 worksheet

Topic: Displacement and force measurement

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] State the variable and reference frame.
- [ ] Choose two points and a consistent measuring method.
- [ ] Measure three times with the instrument reset.
- [ ] Compute the example tilt and compare resolution to expected motion.
- [ ] If using a sensor, draw its mounting and how it affects the model.

## After

Required evidence: A measurement plan and three-repeat readings.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
