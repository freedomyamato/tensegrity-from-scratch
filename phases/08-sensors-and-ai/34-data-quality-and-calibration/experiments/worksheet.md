# Lesson 34 worksheet

Topic: Data quality and calibration

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Calculate the slope and offset from the two reference points.
- [ ] Predict the third point before revealing its example reading.
- [ ] Compute the independent check error.
- [ ] Set a course-specific acceptable error based on the intended measurement, not a universal threshold.
- [ ] Log calibration range, version and missing-data handling.

## After

Required evidence: Calibration equation, independent error and metadata record.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
