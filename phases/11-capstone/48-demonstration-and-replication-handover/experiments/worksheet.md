# Lesson 48 worksheet

Topic: Demonstration and replication handover

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Gather drawings, member schedule, test records, costs and inspection notes.
- [ ] Prepare a five-minute explanation using the stated structure.
- [ ] Have a peer reproduce one build step or numerical result.
- [ ] Record questions and repair missing instructions.
- [ ] Complete the rubric; choose your next route or optional research module.

## After

Required evidence: A replication-ready portfolio and peer handover record.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
