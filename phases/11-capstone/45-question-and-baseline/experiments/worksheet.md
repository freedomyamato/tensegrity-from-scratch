# Lesson 45 worksheet

Topic: Question and baseline

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Choose one outcome you can measure.
- [ ] Name the comparator and common conditions.
- [ ] Write what would support, weaken or leave the claim unresolved.
- [ ] Check that the tools can resolve the expected difference.
- [ ] Define scope and explicitly excluded conclusions.

## After

Required evidence: A predeclared capstone question and evidence criteria.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
