# Lesson 47 worksheet

Topic: Results and limitations

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Present raw values including stopped or missing trials.
- [ ] Calculate mean and range for each comparable metric.
- [ ] Describe instrument resolution and uncontrolled differences.
- [ ] State a conclusion restricted to tested specimens and conditions.
- [ ] Name one next experiment that would reduce an important uncertainty.

## After

Required evidence: A results table, limitations section and next-test proposal.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
