# Lesson 46 worksheet

Topic: Prototype and test plan

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Assign specimen and build IDs.
- [ ] Set common dimensions/load conditions and list unmatched factors.
- [ ] Choose three repeat trials and an alternating sequence.
- [ ] Specify instruments, resolution and stop conditions.
- [ ] Have a peer check that the plan could be repeated, then perform only the bounded trial.

## After

Required evidence: A reviewed test plan and complete trial record.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
