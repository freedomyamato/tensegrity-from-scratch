# Lesson 10 worksheet

Topic: Build a three-strut prism

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Read builds/three-strut-prism.md completely and inspect your joint samples.
- [ ] Mark bottom node positions and rotated top positions on the jig.
- [ ] Place struts along 0–4, 1–5 and 2–3 while the jig supports them.
- [ ] Attach bottom and top triangle cables, then C0,C1,C2; snug gradually in small symmetric increments.
- [ ] Remove support only if joints hold; record all node spans and photograph the model.

## After

Required evidence: A measured model or a documented unsuccessful attempt and diagnosis.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
