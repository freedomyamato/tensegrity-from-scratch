# Lesson 12 worksheet

Topic: Repeatable assembly and joints

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Write a numbered assembly procedure with one check per step.
- [ ] Add node labels and a joint close-up.
- [ ] Disassemble only when the low-tension model can be released safely.
- [ ] Rebuild using the procedure, or ask a partner to use it.
- [ ] Compare three dimensions, time and any interpretation questions.

## After

Required evidence: Revision 2 assembly instructions and repeatability table.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
