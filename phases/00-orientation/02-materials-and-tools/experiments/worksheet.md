# Lesson 02 worksheet

Topic: Materials and tools

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Inventory each item and state its role.
- [ ] Inspect strut ends and remove sharp or damaged parts.
- [ ] Make one low-tension sample joint; mark its start position.
- [ ] Measure its node span, overall length and repeatability.
- [ ] Write a bill of materials using templates/bill-of-materials.csv.

## After

Required evidence: A materials list and a photographed, measured sample joint.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
