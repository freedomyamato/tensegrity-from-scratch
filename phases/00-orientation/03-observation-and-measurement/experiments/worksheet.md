# Lesson 03 worksheet

Topic: Observation and measurement

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Define the two measurement endpoints with tape marks.
- [ ] Read the span without pulling the cord harder each time.
- [ ] Remove and replace the ruler for three readings.
- [ ] Calculate mean and range: maximum minus minimum.
- [ ] Exchange instructions with another person or remeasure after a break; compare.

## After

Required evidence: Three readings, endpoint sketch, mean, range and instrument resolution.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
