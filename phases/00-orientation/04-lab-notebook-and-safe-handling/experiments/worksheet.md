# Lesson 04 worksheet

Topic: Lab notebook and safe handling

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Copy templates/experiment.md into a private local folder.
- [ ] Assign a specimen ID and write your prediction.
- [ ] Record dimensions, boundary conditions, tools and one stop condition.
- [ ] Gently repeat the sample-joint handling test from lesson 02.
- [ ] Log what occurred, including missing measurements; choose one next action.

## After

Required evidence: A completed experiment record with a version ID and stop condition.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
