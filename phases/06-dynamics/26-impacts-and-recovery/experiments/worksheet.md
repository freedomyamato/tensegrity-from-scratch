# Lesson 26 worksheet

Topic: Impacts and recovery

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Record initial geometry and inspect joints.
- [ ] Set a very small displacement that stays within gentle handling; record it.
- [ ] Release without pushing and record settling behavior.
- [ ] Repeat three times after inspection, stopping on slip or damage.
- [ ] Compare final span and joint witness marks with the initial record.

## After

Required evidence: A disturbance protocol and three-trial recovery table.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
