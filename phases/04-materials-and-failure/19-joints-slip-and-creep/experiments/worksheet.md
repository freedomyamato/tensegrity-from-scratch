# Lesson 19 worksheet

Topic: Joints, slip and creep

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Mark both sides of one attachment and record initial span.
- [ ] Keep a small, unchanged initial tension and note the method.
- [ ] Read span and marks at 0,2,5 and 10 minutes.
- [ ] Release tension gently and record whether length recovers.
- [ ] Compare the observations with competing explanations.

## After

Required evidence: A span-versus-time record and diagnosis with uncertainty.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
