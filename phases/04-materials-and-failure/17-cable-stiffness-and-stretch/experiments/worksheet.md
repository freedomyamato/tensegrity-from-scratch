# Lesson 17 worksheet

Topic: Cable stiffness and stretch

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Plot force against extension from the sample dataset.
- [ ] Calculate the slope over two intervals.
- [ ] Predict force at 3 mm extension under the local linear assumption.
- [ ] If testing a real sample, use small increments and stop on slip or damage.
- [ ] Record loading and unloading separately and compare with the ideal law.

## After

Required evidence: A force–extension plot/table and scope of the stiffness estimate.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
