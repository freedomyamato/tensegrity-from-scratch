# Lesson 24 worksheet

Topic: Sensitivity and physical validation

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Change radius by +1% and predict ring length change.
- [ ] Run the geometry and verify that proportional change.
- [ ] Measure three spans of a physical model, or explicitly use synthetic values.
- [ ] Compute absolute and relative differences from prediction.
- [ ] Choose one plausible cause and a measurement that could discriminate it.

## After

Required evidence: Sensitivity result and measured/predicted error table.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
