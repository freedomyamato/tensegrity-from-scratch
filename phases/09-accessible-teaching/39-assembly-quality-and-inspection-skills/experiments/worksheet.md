# Lesson 39 worksheet

Topic: Assembly quality and inspection skills

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Read the written span criterion aloud or show it visually.
- [ ] Measure each sample and record the value.
- [ ] Classify pass or hold without hiding borderline uncertainty.
- [ ] Describe the defect and the required review or adjustment.
- [ ] Have another person audit one record against the sample.

## After

Required evidence: Three inspection entries and one audited record.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
