# Lesson 38 worksheet

Topic: Sensory and motor adaptations

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Name one core learning objective and one nonessential task demand.
- [ ] Offer two support choices and a way to request a change.
- [ ] Provide a quiet or low-stimulation alternative where possible.
- [ ] Let the learner demonstrate understanding in a chosen form.
- [ ] Record which supports helped without collecting unnecessary diagnostic information.

## After

Required evidence: An adapted activity with a concept-aligned assessment.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
