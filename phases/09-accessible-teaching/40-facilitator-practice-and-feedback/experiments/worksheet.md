# Lesson 40 worksheet

Topic: Facilitator practice and feedback

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Choose one objective from lesson 05 or 09.
- [ ] Prepare a short explanation, activity and two teach-back questions.
- [ ] Offer response and support choices.
- [ ] Run the session with a willing peer or rehearse and label it as rehearsal.
- [ ] Collect one clarity comment and revise the next session.

## After

Required evidence: A session plan, observation and revised facilitation note.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
