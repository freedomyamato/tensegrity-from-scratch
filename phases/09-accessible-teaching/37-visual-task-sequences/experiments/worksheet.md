# Lesson 37 worksheet

Topic: Visual task sequences

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Choose one short assembly segment with three or four actions.
- [ ] Make a card for each action with labels and completion cue.
- [ ] Include a help request and pause option.
- [ ] Try the cards with a willing learner or peer using their preferred supports.
- [ ] Record where the sequence was unclear and revise one card.

## After

Required evidence: A tested short task-card sequence.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
