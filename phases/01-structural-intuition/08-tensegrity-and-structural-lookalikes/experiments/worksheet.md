# Lesson 08 worksheet

Topic: Tensegrity and structural lookalikes

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Mark every node and member in both structures.
- [ ] Label cables, struts and any rigid connected frames.
- [ ] Trace the compression connectivity and external supports.
- [ ] Write a classification with the definition stated first.
- [ ] Explain both designs without describing either as magic or antigravity.

## After

Required evidence: A comparison table of nodes, members, supports and definitions.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
