# Lesson 05 worksheet

Topic: Tension versus compression

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Gently pull the string and draw forces at its two ends.
- [ ] Move the ends together and observe slack.
- [ ] Gently compress a sponge to represent compression.
- [ ] Draw end forces on a strut without bending it to failure.
- [ ] Label three components in a reference prism as tension or compression members.

## After

Required evidence: Two force sketches and an annotated prism image.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
