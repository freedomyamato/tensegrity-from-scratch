# Lesson 31 worksheet

Topic: Floating systems and moorings

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Calculate displacement for illustrative total masses 1 and 2 kg.
- [ ] Draw floats, support geometry, mooring points and electrical route as separate systems.
- [ ] Consider tilt, water-level changes, debris and access for repair.
- [ ] List conditions not captured by the tabletop prism.
- [ ] Prepare a basin-test proposal for qualified review rather than placing equipment on open water.

## After

Required evidence: A system diagram and displacement calculation.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
