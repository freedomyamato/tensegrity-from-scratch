# Lesson 30 worksheet

Topic: Rooftop load paths and maintenance

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Draw a fictional roof with parapet, drain and access route.
- [ ] Place a dummy concept and mark gravity/uplift transfer points.
- [ ] Draw the panel-replacement route and a way to inspect joints.
- [ ] List missing roof, attachment, electrical and ownership information.
- [ ] Mark review gates and responsible professional roles without inventing approvals.

## After

Required evidence: An annotated fictional roof concept and review checklist.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
