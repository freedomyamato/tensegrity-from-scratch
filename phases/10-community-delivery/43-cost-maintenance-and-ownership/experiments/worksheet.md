# Lesson 43 worksheet

Topic: Cost, maintenance and ownership

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Enter quantities and actual or explicitly assumed unit costs.
- [ ] Add assembly hours separately from materials.
- [ ] Name likely replacement components and inspection work.
- [ ] Assign an owner, maintainer and repair decision-maker.
- [ ] Compare initial and lifecycle estimates with the baseline frame.

## After

Required evidence: A cost estimate and responsibility matrix.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
