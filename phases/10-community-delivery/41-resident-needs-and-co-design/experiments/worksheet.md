# Lesson 41 worksheet

Topic: Resident needs and co-design

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] List three stakeholder roles and a plausible need for each.
- [ ] Mark each need as assumption or actual feedback.
- [ ] Translate needs into observable requirements, such as an unobstructed route.
- [ ] Identify conflicts and one question to resolve each.
- [ ] Propose a small tabletop co-design session with participation options.

## After

Required evidence: A stakeholder/requirements table with evidence status.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
