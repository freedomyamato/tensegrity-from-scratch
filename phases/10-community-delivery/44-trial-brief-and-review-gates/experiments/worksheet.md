# Lesson 44 worksheet

Topic: Trial brief and review gates

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Define one tabletop trial question and its limit.
- [ ] Assign who facilitates and who checks components.
- [ ] Write two measurable acceptance criteria and stop conditions.
- [ ] List evidence needed before a larger or public stage.
- [ ] Make a go/no-go decision from the available record, marking missing evidence.

## After

Required evidence: A bounded trial brief with gate decisions.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
