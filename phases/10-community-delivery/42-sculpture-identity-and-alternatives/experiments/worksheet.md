# Lesson 42 worksheet

Topic: Sculpture identity and alternatives

## Before

Record date, specimen/activity ID, prediction and setup.

## During

- [ ] Sketch three concepts with different structural or use choices.
- [ ] Annotate panel/dummy-panel area, public interaction and access.
- [ ] Compare each with three requirements from lesson 41.
- [ ] Mark feasibility questions rather than inventing answers.
- [ ] Choose one for the next prototype and state why.

## After

Required evidence: Three concept sketches and selection reasoning.

Record raw observations, units, disagreements, support used and one next step. Do not invent missing data.
