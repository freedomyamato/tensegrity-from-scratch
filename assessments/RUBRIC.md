# Evidence rubric

| Dimension | 0 — missing | 1 — developing | 2 — demonstrated | 3 — repeatable and transferable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Explanation | No explanation | Repeats words with major confusion | Correct explanation using an example | Explains a changed example and limitations |
| Reproducibility | No setup/instructions | Ambiguous parts or methods | Labeled parts, setup and steps | Another person reproduces the task/result |
| Measurement | No values/criteria | Missing units or reference | Raw readings, units, resolution/criteria | Repeats and uncertainty considered |
| Reasoning | Unsupported conclusion | Assumptions hidden | Evidence separated from inference | Competing explanations and discriminating next test |
| Communication | Evidence cannot be located | Partial record | Clear artifact and teach-back in suitable form | Usable handover with feedback incorporated |

Support choices do not automatically lower a score. Assess the stated objective; a learner may demonstrate a concept by pointing while using a partner for an unrelated motor task. For calculation-only tasks, measurement may mean input provenance and numerical checks; mark that form clearly.

Recommended phase progression: level 2 or higher in every applicable dimension and no unresolved task-handling issue. The facilitator records reviewer, evidence and next action. This is a course design choice, not a validated psychometric scale.
