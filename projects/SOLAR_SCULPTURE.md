# One estate, one solar sculpture — tabletop capstone

## Question

Can a small tensegrity support offer useful adjustability or assembly/maintenance benefits compared with a conventional frame supporting the same light dummy panel? Decide which benefit you will measure before building. Do not assume energy or structural superiority.

## Scope

Use a light card panel first. Model an estate identity through drawings or a decorative base that does not obscure the structural labels. Keep the model on a contained tabletop; no roof, public installation or full-size panel is part of this capstone.

## Procedure

1. Define common panel area, target angle, footprint and benign indoor conditions.
2. Choose three metrics: assembly time, measured mass, angle drift, repair access or repeated-assembly variation. State their measurement method.
3. Build a low-tension support and a simple frame baseline. Record any differences you cannot control.
4. Perform at least three bounded rebuild/measurement trials with alternating order. Retain failures and stop conditions.
5. Summarize raw values, mean/range and uncertainty. Explain when the frame performs better.
6. Prepare a short community workshop with labeled parts, help/pause options and a nonverbal teach-back alternative.
7. Have a peer reproduce one build step or calculation using only your instructions.

## Optional real solar extension

Only after the mechanical tabletop activity is suitable, consider a small educational low-voltage panel and its specified load. Follow device instructions; no grid connection. Match acquisition conditions or alternate measurements, log illumination/orientation/shading and integrate simultaneous loaded V,I readings. An observed yield difference does not alone establish a change in conversion efficiency.

## Acceptance and handover

Success means a documented comparison, repeatable method and explicit limits. It does not require outperforming the baseline. Deliver geometry, joints, materials/costs, raw data, inspection plan, concept artwork and workshop pack. State which stakeholder needs were assumed and which came from actual consented feedback.

Full-scale next steps require actual-site professional structural/electrical review, permissions and maintenance ownership. Keep that separate from the tabletop outcome.
