# Community learning kit

## Question

Can another facilitator use your kit to help a learner identify tension/compression and reproduce one labeled connection?

## Build the kit

Include an inspected tabletop model, labeled component samples, node diagram, one-action task cards, a clear finished example, ruler, notebook and inspection form. Include a drawing-only or preassembled option so knot tying is not an unnecessary barrier to mechanics learning.

Choose supports with the learner: larger labels, quiet workspace, short steps, pauses, jigs or partner help. Do not promise therapeutic benefit or assume adoption by Pathlight/ARC. Engage relevant educators and participants for any real institutional pilot.

## Pilot and evaluate

First rehearse with a peer, marking it as rehearsal. Then, where appropriate and consented, deliver a short session. Record whether the learner identified a cable/strut and followed one connection card. Collect clarity feedback, not unnecessary personal or diagnostic data. Revise one card and repeat the task if the participant wants to continue.

## Handover

Deliver kit inventory, inspection procedure, 90-minute plan or revised timing, task cards, objective-aligned rubric and non-identifying feedback. A facilitator replication attempt completes the project. Outcome claims must name the actual pilot scope; do not generalize a single session to all learners.
