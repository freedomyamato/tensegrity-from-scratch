# What the course does and does not establish

## Educational mechanics

The geometry lab uses point nodes, ideal axial members, stated connectivity and unloaded self-stress. It checks member lengths, force-density signs and equilibrium residual. It does not compute full elastic stability, second-order rigidity, nonlinear constitutive response, loaded support reactions, joint capacity, fatigue or structural design margins.

The spring, Euler column, oscillator, solar-energy integrator and mock controller each have distinct assumptions. Read their docstrings and lab notes. Changing an input is a mathematical exploration, not permission to increase a real model’s load.

## Physical activities

Use blunt lightweight classroom materials, low tension and a contained work surface. Avoid high-energy elastic release, cutting loaded members, unreviewed motors, roofs and open water. Suitable supervision depends on tools, participants and context. A facilitator can substitute drawings or calculations when a physical task is unsuitable.

The build procedure is a proposed classroom method. Its nominal geometry is numerically checked, but no physical assembly, classroom timing or durability test was performed for this release. Pilot gently and record mismatches, joint behavior and learner feedback.

## Solar and installation claims

Support geometry can affect orientation, shading and temperature; it does not inherently increase solar-cell conversion efficiency. Compare loaded power and energy under controlled conditions. Full-scale HDB roofs, floating solar arrays and public structures require actual-site professional review, permission, applicable current requirements and maintenance ownership. This course neither grants those permissions nor invents the site-specific rules.

## Education and research

Accessibility options are proposals to choose with learners and relevant educators. No therapeutic outcome, Pathlight/ARC adoption, employment placement or accredited certification is claimed. Quantum Tensegrity remains an optional research hypothesis; classical tensegrity analogy does not establish a theory of entanglement or gravity.
